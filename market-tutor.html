    Learn: Candle body as a percentage of its range

    The open is the starting price and the close is the ending price for a candle. The high is its highest price and the low is its lowest. The body is the distance from open to close; the full range is the distance from low to high.

    Steps: 1. Subtract open from close and use the positive distance. 2. Subtract low from high. 3. Divide body by range. 4. Multiply by 100 to get a percentage. Example: open $2.00, close $2.06, high $2.10, low $1.90 → $0.06 ÷ $0.20 × 100 = 30%.

    Try the example, then change the close to $2.00 to discover what a doji means.

    This is a fictional example, not a historical chart or a prediction. A larger body alone does not establish a profitable trade. Compare the candle with volume, market context and nearby levels.
