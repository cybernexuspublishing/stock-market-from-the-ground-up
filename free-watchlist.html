Talia’s watchlist rule

Before adding a symbol, answer: Who published this? When? What can be verified? What is missing? A screenshot, high relative volume or a scanner hit does not establish a dependable catalyst, executable liquidity or a future price move.

In a fictional example, GUSIM can be marked FICTIONAL and used for paper-only observation. A real ticker with an unsupported rumor belongs in WAITING FOR SOURCE or NO TRADE until independently sourced evidence changes the decision.
