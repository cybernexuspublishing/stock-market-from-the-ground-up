Gate 1: Truth Behind the Ticker and Lab Batch B01

The Owner has approved all seven Boot Camps and all 419 canonical Lab entries for Founding Member beta testing. This includes checking unavailable or Upcoming content and reporting exact defects; it does not certify every lesson as finished or permit unreviewed grading. Founding Member testing is open for accessible Guided Beta lessons and Boot Camp study. Begin with the five original source-verification investigations and twenty individually authored B01 Labs, then explore the other source-backed Labs in the selected 336 and the newly authored Site-Exclusive Guided Beta studies SITE-018 through SITE-100 where your learning access permits. Try the nine learning stages, practice questions, Talia guidance, save and resume, and report missing teaching or broken controls. Guided Beta is ungraded: only separately reviewed assessments can earn verified assessment credit. If a lesson is unavailable to your account, report the exact Lab ID and entitlement message; no unavailable activity is ever counted as a pass. No LIVE payment or in-site brokerage simulation is involved.

  1. Start and resume: Open the five-day Truth Behind the Ticker Boot Camp. Complete each day’s original evidence investigation, save a written reflection, sign out, return, and verify that the saved work and day status are accurate.
  2. Unsaved privacy: Draft two separate day answers, move between days, then change accounts or reload. Saved responses must persist for the correct account; unsaved local answers must disappear on account change/reload.
  3. Book entitlement: Open an authorized Book I Batch B01 Lab and Book II Batch B01 Lab. Verify that a book not owned remains restricted, even when a Boot Camp is accessible.
  4. Teach, then assess: For every released Lab, verify the instructor teaches before questioning, the chart matches the question, free response and guided remediation work, and an invalid or unsupported answer never earns mastery.
  5. Review and retake: Where reviewed graded questions are released, try correct, incorrect and retry routes; confirm feedback explains the evidence and limitations without exposing the answer key before submission.
  6. Accessible on mobile and desktop: Check keyboard focus, Talia answer entry, the nine learning stages, reading order and layouts at mobile and desktop widths; report any clipped chart, unreadable label or lost keyboard control.

When reporting a problem, include the Boot Camp day or canonical Lab ID, your browser and device, the expected and actual behavior, and reproducible steps. Report private account or grade discrepancies through Support, not a public forum. Do not post assessment keys, passwords, member records or payment details.

How to submit a useful beta report

State the area or page, the steps to reproduce, expected result, actual result, device and browser, and whether the issue happens again. Include a Lab ID when relevant. Do not share passwords, email addresses, payment details, private account records, or unreleased grading answers in a community post.
