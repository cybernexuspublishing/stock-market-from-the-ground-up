Advertising standards

  • Every placement is visibly labeled Advertisement or Sponsored.
  • No ads inside ENTER/WATCH/NO TRADE decision prompts, Tutor answers, order-entry controls, P&L, or risk warnings.
  • No guaranteed-return claims, pump groups, deceptive signal services, unlawful offerings, or misleading financial promotions.
  • Full Monthly and Full Annual may contain limited sponsor placements; Lifetime and OWNER access remain ad-free.

All advertising is subject to editorial review, placement availability, member access controls, and a written campaign agreement. Published placements will not begin until payment is independently verified. Rates are provided by custom quote; this inquiry form does not request payment information or create a purchase.

How direct sponsorship works: Submit a business inquiry; receive an individually reviewed written quote and campaign terms; and, once the campaign is approved, receive a secure Stripe invoice to your verified business email. You pay through Stripe's hosted invoice page. Advertisements are published only after independent payment confirmation and final creative approval. No subscription or automatic renewal is created by an inquiry.
