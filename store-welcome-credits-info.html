CROWNlight LUMENS · PRELAUNCH INFORMATION

Welcome Credits, Trials and Expiry

What the proposed Crownlight Lumens welcome offer means, how credits may be redeemed, and which access benefits have an expiration date.

The proposed welcome-credit offer

Upcoming: 286 complimentary Crownlight Lumens after eligible account registration and email verification. At the currently planned platform redemption rate of $0.07 per Lumen, that represents up to $20.02 toward qualifying future product purchases. This is not a cash payment and is not currently activated or issued.

When activated, a qualifying member may receive the welcome award once, after email verification and eligible registration. Promotional credits will be recorded separately from purchased, earned and membership-included credits. The offer is subject to final publication and account eligibility requirements.

Using Lumens toward a purchase

Where a product and its checkout are authorized for Lumen redemption, members may choose a whole number of Lumens to apply, from zero to the lesser of their current balance or the amount needed to cover the eligible subtotal. The member must be shown their credit use, remaining balance and any cash due before confirming.

For example, on an eligible $19.99 product, applying 40 Lumens at $0.07 each gives a $2.80 discount and leaves $17.19 before separately assessed taxes, shipping and fees. Applying 80 Lumens gives a $5.60 discount and leaves $14.39. If enough Lumens are available, eligible products may be fully covered. Whole-credit rounding means the last Lumen can cover less than seven cents without cash change.

Mixed cash-and-Lumen checkout is still in development. Credit-bundle checkout is disabled during pricing reconciliation. No purchase becomes available simply because it is described here.

Do Lumens expire?

Unused Lumens do not expire under the current Crownlight policy. However, a temporary service, subscription period, day/week/month pass or trial purchased using Lumens can expire at its stated end date. This is expiration of the acquired access—not expiration of the remaining credit balance.

Standalone fixed-term trials and passes do not automatically renew unless a separate purchase or expressly disclosed subscription contract is entered into. A benefit already included with the member's active membership should not be charged a second time.

Can Lumens be exchanged for money?

No. Crownlight Lumens are limited-use, non-cash platform credits. They cannot be cashed out, exchanged for real money, withdrawn, sold back to the platform, or converted to cryptocurrency or brokerage funds. The $0.07 rate is a platform discount calculation, not a cash exchange rate. Refunds or remedies legally required for an underlying transaction are preserved.

Where are the binding terms?

Read the Crownlight Lumens Purchase & Use Terms, Terms of Service and Refund and Cancellation Policy. If a policy page or product offer differs from this educational overview, the currently published applicable terms and checkout disclosures govern, subject to law.
