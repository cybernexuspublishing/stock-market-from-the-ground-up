Tiingo — Bring Your Own Token

Important: Tiingo's free accounts are intended for individual internal/personal usage; they do not authorize redistribution to other members or publishing shared commercial market-data content. Each member must hold their own valid account, abide by usage limits, and not register duplicate accounts to evade quotas. See Tiingo's official developer terms.

Use your own Tiingo account/API token. The token is encrypted before storage when the Cloudflare MARKET_DATA_ENCRYPTION_KEY secret is configured. The browser never receives the stored token again.

Alpaca — Personal Market Data (IEX)

Your own Alpaca Paper Trading API Key ID and Secret also provide access to Alpaca's Market Data API, subject to your account's data permissions. You do not need a second key pair or an endpoint field. The website uses data.alpaca.markets automatically, tests an IEX historical candle, and keeps the encrypted keys private to your account.

Paper orders and live brokerage orders are not part of this connection. IEX data are from a single exchange, not a consolidated SIP market feed.

Data-rights standard

A provider connection does not give the platform permission to redistribute that provider's data to other users. BYO-token connections are used under the individual user's provider entitlement. Curated teaching datasets carry their own provenance and licensing record.
