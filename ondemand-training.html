Historical Replay Training

Practice the books' decision-making sequence using an independently authorized thinkorswim OnDemand installation. We provide original instructions and structured reflection; we do not provide the trading software, market data, or a brokerage account.

Before starting: Confirm OnDemand supports the ticker, date, and tools for the selected exercise. Never advance past the decision timestamp until you have written and locked your answer. Playback availability may vary. This learning platform is independent of Charles Schwab and thinkorswim.
