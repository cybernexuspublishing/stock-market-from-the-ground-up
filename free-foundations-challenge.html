Your five-day learning path

These are the five actual Free Foundations topics taught by Talia Mercer in the member Academy. You can study at your own pace; a day means a lesson, not a mandatory consecutive calendar day.

  1. 01

    What is a stock, and what does the market do?

    Tell an ownership share apart from a quote, ticker, order and completed trade.

    Day 1 lesson
  2. 02

    How to read a five-minute candlestick

    Label open, high, low and close, then distinguish a candle's body from its full range.

    Day 2 lesson
  3. 03

    Claims, catalysts and what was actually known

    Separate a primary source and its timestamp from a repost, interpretation or later price move.

    Day 3 lesson
  4. 04

    Make a watchlist, not a list of trade signals

    Organize observations, source dates, reasons to monitor and criteria for removing a candidate.

    Day 4 lesson
  5. 05

    Plan risk, choose NO TRADE and review your work

    Work through a fictional position-size model, execution uncertainty and a defensible no-trade plan.

    Day 5 lesson
