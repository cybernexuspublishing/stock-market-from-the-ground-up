Reproducible decision training

No-look-ahead discipline

Historical exercises remain anchored to ticker, date, ET time boundary, timeframe, studies, and the information available at that point.

Failure and restraint

Failed setups, ambiguity, and NO TRADE are valid learning outcomes. The system is designed to reward disciplined reasoning rather than hindsight.

Adaptive practice

Your recorded mistake tags and saved attempts drive recommendations about what to practice next. The system becomes more personalized as your record grows.

Portable visual + historical evidence

Conceptual market-mechanics topics may use diagrams. Market-behavior topics keep the portable in-book visual and use reproducible historical-market exercises when applicable. The website adds guided education, but it does not execute or simulate brokerage orders. External historical order practice takes place in thinkorswim OnDemand.
