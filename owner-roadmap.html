Tracking and verification policy

Delegated project review: The Owner has authorized delegated technical assessment. Checked baseline items approve only the documented delivered control, not entire systems. Implemented-source items remain pending production testing. These decisions are version-controlled and are not falsely attributed to an authenticated Owner database update. An Owner-saved decision always overrides the baseline. No payment, security, account role, data rights, legal, or publication authority is delegated by these checklist statuses.

Engineering review is a dated, read-only assessment of the implementation and available evidence. It is separate from Owner checkboxes and never makes a feature live or changes a status you have already saved.

Owner marked complete means a signed-in Owner has manually completed the checklist item. Evidence verified means the Owner recorded independent acceptance evidence in the task note. Neither status enables checkout, changes entitlements, applies database migrations, publishes books, or deploys releases.

Source coverage: historical project commitments, the 77-step acceptance register, preserved promise matrix, current Stripe work, and 1,815 line-level master/advanced-engineering trace records. Repetitions are retained in the trace view to avoid silently omitting historical instructions.
