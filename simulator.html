Market Simulator

Replay OHLCV candles one bar at a time, lock the plan before entry, simulate a long or short trade, and save the result directly into the Trading Journal. Future candles remain hidden.

Simulation model: This version uses candle OHLCV data, not historical Level II or tick-by-tick order flow. Market entries/exits use the current candle close unless a stop/target is triggered. If a candle touches both the stop and target, the simulator pauses because OHLC alone cannot determine which occurred first.
