Build a repeatable market-learning workflow

Start by observing a security and recording the evidence, rather than following a signal. A watchlist helps organize research; a trade plan defines entry conditions, invalidation, and risk; chart practice helps you examine price and volume; and a decision journal records what you observed and why you acted or chose not to trade.
