Release controls and inventory

Direct sponsorships are sold by an approved written quote. Billing opens only the Owner-directed Stripe LIVE invoice workflow. Displaying paid ads remains a separate approval.

Page advertising visibility

Turn off every placement on an individual page, including unsold house-promotion cards and the Knowledge Database TradingView banner. Switching a page back on enables all its placements. This never enables LIVE ad billing or approves a campaign.

Rate card & packages

Default rates are deliberately unset. Enter a contract rate only after approving real prices; published rates do not generate a Stripe charge.

Advertiser inquiries

New inquiries are private. Review them before creating an advertiser; never publish a contact email on the website.

Placements & Creatives

No remote tracking pixel or third-party embedded script is allowed. Only text and an explicitly reviewed HTTPS destination are accepted.

Campaign approvals and direct sponsorship billing

Approve the advertiser, contract and creative first. For an approved campaign, the Owner may issue and email a Stripe LIVE invoice. The advertiser pays on Stripe's hosted invoice page; payment must be independently verified before publication. A sent invoice does not activate the advertisement.

The Owner must confirm the written quote, flight dates, billing contact, permitted advertising content and billing authorization before sending the invoice. Do not send invoices for test records.

Sales performance (recorded events only)

Impression and click counts are observational. No CPC/CPM customer billing is enabled and missing records are not estimated.
