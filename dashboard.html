      What is included in Free, and when do advanced tools become relevant?

      Free members can study five ungraded Foundations lessons, practice with original fictional candles, maintain a manual research watchlist and save Journal entries within their existing allowance. Explanations, examples and guided teaching never require Sparks.

      Connected provider data, protected historical replay and other advanced tools require their own approved eligibility and released functionality. A paid entitlement does not create a licensed dataset or imply a broker execution. You can continue learning without upgrading.
