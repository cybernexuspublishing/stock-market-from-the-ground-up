## Proposed consumer-facing text

### Scope
Cookies, web storage and related technologies are used by the website and approved service providers. Some are required to establish secure sign-in, protect against abuse, retain authentication state, keep page navigation accessible, and respect interface preferences. Examples of preferences may include text size, instructor text size, visual scaling, selected tabs and member display settings.

### Essential technologies
Authentication and security state, CSRF prevention, anti-abuse challenges, load/performance safeguards, and session continuity may need device/browser storage. Clearing essential storage can log out the user or interrupt features. An actual itemized cookie register must identify each deployed name, owner, purpose, expiration and whether it is necessary.

### Optional analytics and advertisements
Optional analytics, campaign measurement, personalisation, interest-based advertising or third-party advertising technology are not automatically essential. Their use must respect applicable consent, notice and opt-out laws. Only deploy optional scripts when the required legal basis and controls are in place. Payment providers and embedded third-party services may apply their own technologies under their separate notices; our integration should minimize unnecessary exposure.

### User controls
Where optional storage choices are offered, they should be accessible through appropriate Privacy/Consent settings and operate before applicable trackers fire. Browser settings may block or remove cookies, though essential functionality can stop working. Browser signals and statutory opt-out requests will be honored where applicable and technologically supported, after verification.

### Updates and inventory
Changes in a vendor, purpose or tracking category may require updated disclosures and renewed choice. The final public notice must be synchronized with a verified technical inventory, not merely copied from this draft.
