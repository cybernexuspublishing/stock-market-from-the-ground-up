## Proposed consumer-facing text

### Data controller and covered systems
This statement applies to information collected by The Stock Market: From the Ground Up and the first-party site account, educational content, Market Commons, Journal, support, Store and payment interactions. The Operator's verified contracting legal name and privacy contact must be disclosed in the final published notice. Payment processing may be provided by a separately identified merchant of record, including Stripe via Link for eligible purchases; that provider may act as an independent controller for its own legally mandated functions.

### Information categories and sources
Information processed to operate services can include your account name, username, email, protected date of birth or age eligibility, login/session and security identifiers, device/network logs, preferences, account access roles, educational actions and competency evidence, tutor prompts and answers, practice and Journal records, community posts, moderation reports, support messages, and purchase/order/subscription data. Payment providers process card and payment details directly; we may receive limited payment identifiers, pricing, status, fraud/dispute signals and receipts. Connected market-data credentials, if supported, are sensitive user-supplied information and should not be shown publicly.

### Use purposes
We process appropriate data to register and authenticate adults, preserve learning progress, deliver entitlements, reconcile orders, operate course tools, support customers, enforce safety and anti-abuse rules, diagnose outages, fulfill reporting duties, comply with law, and administer optional marketing or advertising where supported and authorized. Do not represent behavioral profiles or trading ideas as licensed professional advice. An advertiser does not receive private tutor messages or payment credentials merely by purchasing an ad.

### Disclosures and service providers
Infrastructure and transactional service providers may include Supabase, Cloudflare, Stripe/Link, email communications providers, marketplace/print distributors and member-selected data connections. Each receives only the information reasonably necessary for its function under its agreements and applicable law. Actual subprocessor names, roles, hosting jurisdictions, analytics tooling, cookie identifiers and overseas transfers must be verified and documented prior to final publication. Community posts and voluntary public profile information are visible as described by their audience settings.

### Optional features, AI and ads
The educational tutor may process member prompts and interaction results as necessary to provide guidance, monitor quality and prevent misuse. Any external AI providers and their retention/training choices must be disclosed accurately. Optional analytics, tracking, targeted advertising and marketing communications require the notices, legal bases, consent/opt-out mechanisms and configuration applicable to each jurisdiction. Do not claim that data is never shared, never trained on or never transferred overseas without auditing service contracts and settings.

### Retention and deletion
Keep identifiable data only for documented business, educational, security, financial, tax and legal retention purposes. Some order, chargeback, fraud and audit records must survive account closure when legally necessary. A deletion request does not entitle the requester to erase evidence that law requires the business to retain, but remaining personal information should be minimized and access-restricted. A verified retention schedule must identify categories, retention periods, legitimate exceptions and deletion jobs.

### Privacy and account requests
Adults may request access, correction, export, deletion, marketing preference changes or other rights where law grants them. The Support Center and privacy contact should authenticate sensitive requests without collecting excessive additional information. Rights vary by jurisdiction, and any applicable appeal, opt-out, authorized-agent, sensitive-data and nonretaliation mechanisms must be implemented before claiming availability. We will not condition lawful rights upon abandoning membership claims or waiving disputes.

### Security and incidents
Reasonable measures should include TLS, least-privilege administration, row-level authorization, private document storage, secure credentials, logging, incident response, supplier review and regular testing. No platform can warrant absolute protection. If an incident triggers notification obligations, notices will follow applicable law and regulator/provider requirements.

### Age and sensitive data
This service is intended for adults aged 18 and older. Do not knowingly open accounts for minors. Preserve restricted treatment of DOB and connected credentials. Avoid collecting unneeded brokerage passwords, SSNs, precise financial-account information or special-category data.

### Cookies and changes
The Cookie Notice details browser-side technologies. Material changes to purposes, providers or rights will be communicated when the law requires and may require fresh consent. The final notice must include an actual effective date and a verified privacy contact.
