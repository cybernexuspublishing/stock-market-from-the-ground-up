## Proposed customer-facing language

### Nature of Sparks
Crownlight Sparks are internal digital credits used for eligible platform education and member experiences. Sparks cannot be redeemed for cash or trading funds or traded outside the website. Member-to-member transfers and gifting are not available until expressly released. Applicable consumer rights remain unaffected.

### Purchase packages
The announced package quantities and prices are 25 Sparks for $5.00, 55 for $10.00, 90 for $15.00, 125 for $20.00, and 275 for $40.00, before applicable tax. Package displays do not constitute active checkout offers. Purchasing remains disabled until verified payment collection, fulfillment, and required disclosures are available.

### Optional learning and advertising features
An eligible ungraded practice answer reveal or question skip costs 4 Sparks after confirmation. Each assisted question remains study-only and does not award grade, certification, or mastery. Eligible advertising-supported members may redeem 5 Sparks for 24 hours, 18 Sparks for 7 days, or 50 Sparks for 30 days without eligible sponsor advertisements. The pass starts upon verified redemption, and subsequent passes extend from the later of the current expiration or the new redemption time. Existing ad-free memberships do not need to redeem these passes. Ad-free passes do not remove required legal, transactional, or account notices.

Core instruction, guided examples, corrections, and ordinary practice retries remain available under the member's applicable entitlements without spending Sparks. Other marketplace offers are available only when separately released and expressly priced.

### Wallet, expiration, and access periods
Unused Crownlight Sparks do not expire, whether purchased, earned, included in a membership, or issued by the operator. This policy does not automatically reinstate credits that expired before its adoption; applicable legal rights and support remedies remain available. Temporary benefits redeemed with Sparks expire after their stated access period. Each redeemed benefit is distinguished from the non-expiring credit balance.

### Payment reversals and adjustments
When checkout is activated, purchases and refunds remain subject to the published Refund Policy, provider requirements, and applicable law. Audited corrections may address demonstrable errors, unauthorized transactions, or abuse; unrelated valid balances must not be arbitrarily confiscated.

### Contact and support
Questions may be submitted through the website Support Center or to stockmarketfromthegroundup@gmail.com.
