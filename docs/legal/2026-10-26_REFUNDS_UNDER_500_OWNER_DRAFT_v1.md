## Proposed customer-facing policy — NOT PUBLISHED
### Purchases under $500
Completed purchases for less than $500.00 USD are ordinarily final once the purchased educational content or digital access is provided. We do not normally grant discretionary refunds merely because a customer changes their mind or does not use delivered services.

### Legally or contractually required exceptions
This policy does not limit any refund, return, cancellation, remedy, or dispute right required by applicable law, card-network rules, provider agreements, or the terms expressly offered at purchase. Reports of unauthorized or fraudulent transactions, duplicate charges, incorrect billing, nondelivery, materially defective access or material misdescription will be investigated. A customer does not have to establish criminal fraud independently before reporting a suspected unauthorized charge or asserting a protected right.

### Purchases of $500 or more
Requests are individually evaluated under the applicable offer, legal requirements and payment-provider rules. The threshold does not guarantee a refund.

### Subscriptions and retailer purchases
Canceling a recurring membership prevents future eligible renewals under the posted terms but does not by itself refund an already completed paid term. Purchases through third-party retailers must follow the appropriate merchant-of-record refund process. Lawful payment disputes remain available. Provider-initiated refunds and reversals must be reconciled to orders, entitlements and tax entries.

### Request a review
Contact the Support Center with the order reference, charge date, amount, and reason. Do not transmit payment-card numbers.

## Mandatory review items
Determine whether the $500 threshold includes sales tax, assess each customer jurisdiction, confirm checkout disclosures and Stripe Managed Payments refund discretion, handle non-delivery and preorders, and preserve documented cancellation and chargeback rights. Do not publish this draft or automatically deny refund requests solely on the amount until legal and provider review is complete.
