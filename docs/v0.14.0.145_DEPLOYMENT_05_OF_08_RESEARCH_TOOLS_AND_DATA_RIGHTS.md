## Defects corrected in research/discovery

- Older scanner treated empty/unknown price, percent change, RVOL, float and spread as passing fields and ranked them with a pseudo-precise score even where evidence was absent. The new scanner **excludes missing required observations**, rejects non-numeric values, rejects invalid symbol/duplicate rows and enforces strict row (2000) and byte (2 MB) limits.
- Removed the arbitrary opaque trading-style "Teaching Score"; results are reproducibly sorted by supplied % change, share volume, then symbol, with exact admitted/rejected counts. No trade-signal or performance-success claim is made.
- A source/rights note and timezone-offset snapshot timestamp are required for private filter-set provenance; these remain **user reported**, not independently approved. No third-party market data are uploaded to the platform by the scanner.
- Watchlist saves require a valid symbol, source note, time-zone-aware observation time and an explicit invalidation or NO TRADE condition. Existing legacy records remain readable and labeled as missing older provenance.
- Catalyst note saves require a valid symbol, bounded original HTTPS URL with no embedded credentials, exact source-time offset, substantive reported fact and uncertainty. This is a research ledger, not real-time market news or an evidentiary proof of causality.
- Pure EMA function uses an SMA seed, explicit period and bounded positive provided close values; it **does not** certify that the prices came from a particular historical market. Typical-price volume-weighted OHLCV indicator explicitly says **approximation**, not trade-derived VWAP or consolidated market-wide volume.

## Severe provider security and free-rights correction

The preexisting `functions/api/marketdata.js` always used `Authorization: Bearer ${SUPABASE_SECRET_KEY}` even when Supabase delivered modern opaque `sb_secret_*` credentials. This is unsafe/incompatible with Supabase's JWT authorization semantics and has been repaired. Key material is server-only.

The BYO adapter now rejects cross-site credential mutations, enforces bounded body and token formats, verifies that the logged-in user is an eligible member, validates requested provider symbols and OHLCV records, bounds upstream transport deadlines, denies unsupported providers, and masks raw provider error messages and tokens from clients.

**Important source license findings (verified October 21, 2026):**
- https://www.tiingo.com/documentation/ — Basic / Power accounts may not redistribute Tiingo data; internal/personal use only.
- https://www.tiingo.com/documentation/appendix/developers — Tiingo permits developers to have **each user supply their own account/token** under its developer program, subject to conditions; this is different from redistributing one shared provider account's data.
- https://app.tiingo.com/tos/ — commercial or organization use, redistribution restrictions, and **one account per person** rules apply. Do NOT register multiple free accounts to bypass limits.
- https://www.iex.io/legal/hist-data-terms — free historical IEX data are venue-limited and any member display must meet IEX's attribution/reuse terms; decoded PCAP still requires real-file technical QA.
- https://www.sec.gov/search-filings/edgar-application-programming-interfaces — public EDGAR issuer submissions are free, not actual stock trades/quotes.

A member's own personal Tiingo token is NEVER an entitlement for the publisher to combine, sell, redistribute or build a shared market-history archive. BYO integration remains a private viewer **for that member only**. No member-specific provider response becomes a public Lab source. Provider's exact commercial SaaS developer agreement should be checked before any paid customer rollout.
