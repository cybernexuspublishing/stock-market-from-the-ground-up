## Production enablement — NOT performed

The Owner may **consider** Workers AI only if the existing Cloudflare account is verified to be a Workers **Free** plan, the model's current terms/license are reviewed, a separate AI binding is added to existing Pages Functions (binding name `AI`), required v103 Supabase atomic quota RPC is installed and verified, and zero-dollar expense controls are confirmed. The adapter will remain off without:
- `AI_TUTOR_PROVIDER=cloudflare_workers_ai`
- `AI_TUTOR_MODEL=@cf/meta/llama-3.1-8b-instruct-fp8`
- `AI_SERVICE_ENABLED=true` (explicit Owner-controlled switch)
- existing positive `AI_SERVICE_MEMBER_DAILY_LIMIT`, `AI_SERVICE_GLOBAL_DAILY_LIMIT`, `AI_SERVICE_GLOBAL_MONTHLY_LIMIT`
- existing valid Supabase project URL and secret available **only** on the server.

**Important cost boundary:** Cloudflare documents a 10,000-Neuron daily free allocation, but actual requests consume variable Neurons. Request-count quotas cannot guarantee a zero-dollar invoice on a paid Workers plan or where other workloads share allowance. Do not enable on a paid plan without a separately validated hard ceiling. Do not convert 10,000 Neurons into a fictional guaranteed number of questions. Provider errors fall back to original source-only teaching.
