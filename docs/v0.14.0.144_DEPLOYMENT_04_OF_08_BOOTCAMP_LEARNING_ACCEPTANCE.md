## Real market and current-law controls

No invented transactions, prices, tape, chart prints, stock-performance probabilities, outcomes or attributed quotes occur in the curriculum. A day may be studied conceptually while historical evidence remains **unavailable**; the learner is not told a source has been approved.

- U.S. standard settlement for most stocks is T+1 since **May 28, 2024**: https://www.investor.gov/introduction-investing/general-resources/news-alerts/alerts-bulletins/investor-bulletins/new-t1-settlement-cycle-what-investors-need-know-investor-bulletin
- FINRA's new intraday margin standards became effective **June 4, 2026**, with permitted broker transition ending **October 20, 2027**: https://www.investor.gov/introduction-investing/general-resources/news-alerts/alerts-bulletins/investor-bulletins/margin
- SEC EDGAR API is free/public, but the primary URL and source timestamp must be documented, and SEC fair access remains applicable: https://www.sec.gov/search-filings/edgar-application-programming-interfaces

Never present 2020 vendor rule explanations as current law, a trade-alarm success anecdote as measured expectancy, reported profits as an executed fill, or one venue's IEX TOPS observations as consolidated tape/NBBO.
