## Implemented

**1. Real public crawler files:** Cloudflare Pages route handlers `functions/robots.txt.js` and `functions/sitemap.xml.js` provide crawler hints and an XML sitemap for eleven specifically verified public HTML pages. Sensitive server APIs, admin/owner/member workspaces, per-member training data and browser-rendered legal shells are excluded from the sitemap. The robots file disallows private areas but **robots is not authentication**. Canonical origin is taken from the validated HTTPS request origin unless an optional, server-only `PUBLIC_SITE_ORIGIN` is set. No owner action or new Cloudflare variable is required for the default route; setting a fixed origin is recommended after owner domain verification.

**2. Do not index misleading legal placeholders:** Existing 10 legal HTML shells have `noindex,follow` until the production policy body is independently reviewed and made visible to crawlers in initial HTML. This does **not** remove policy access; JavaScript continues to load published records from the existing Supabase table. It prevents search results that show only `Loading policy…` or `Policy document unavailable`. The 10 legal pages are TERMS, PRIVACY, SUBSCRIPTION, REFUNDS, DISCLAIMER, COMMUNITY, ADVERTISING, COOKIES, ACCESSIBILITY and COPYRIGHT.

**3. Owner-only legal publication panel:** The Owner Website Development Roadmap now includes a private read-only status check via `GET /api/owner-roadmap?action=legal-publication-v014109`. It inspects those ten `legal_documents` records, including published/nonempty status and length, but does not return the policy body, backend secrets or member information. Empty/missing tables report `not verified`, not certified zero. Merely having a published policy record is expressly not a legal or compliance acceptance.

**4. CI/regressions:** Tests cover XML sitemap allowlisting, exclusion of protected/private routes, HTTPS-only configured origins, sitemap discovery in robots, the ten noindex document shells, denied Admin role access to Owner legal status, modern Supabase opaque key protection, missing-database behavior and non-disclosure of policy bodies.

## Release risks still open

The real domain should be explicitly configured and tested for canonical HTTPS URL accuracy and indexability. The policy HTML shells are **client-rendered**, not server-rendered, and search engines may not ingest a useful policy. Before removing noindex: obtain qualified counsel review of privacy/terms/refund/subscription rules, vendor/model-provider disclosure, retention and erasure, market-data licensing, affiliate/advertising policy, accessibility and trading-education disclaimers; publish reviewed text in crawlable HTML with the currently published policy version. Do not fabricate legal policy or imply compliance based only on text length. Canonical tags, Open Graph tags, social thumbnails, alt text and sitemap discovery remain additional QA.

No SQL, Stripe price, paid entitlement, private book manuscript, market data or customer billing was changed by this phase.
