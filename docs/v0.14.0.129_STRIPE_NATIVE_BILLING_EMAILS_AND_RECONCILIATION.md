## Decision and boundaries

The owner cannot purchase a sender domain and has elected to use **Stripe's native customer billing emails**, not a new Resend account. Stripe sends supported payment/invoice/recovery notifications under its own billing functionality; it does **not** send all custom website membership-access notices from the original v123 template. Do not claim otherwise. Stripe may charge applicable Payments and Billing processing/usage fees; the ordinary Stripe-hosted customer portal does not require a purchased custom domain.

The original v123 notice outbox, notification endpoint and original v128 billing Worker are preserved unchanged in GitHub for auditability and possible future opted-in delivery. The new entrypoint is the standalone `workers/billing-reconcile-stripe-only-v014129.js` and sends no custom emails. It intentionally calls only `/api/internal-billing-reconcile-v014124` once per Cron execution. No member entitlement is changed by this Cron; only verified signed LIVE webhook processing grants/revokes access. No new database migrations, table drops, mass outbox ack/dead-letter changes, or provider calls are necessary.

**Operational limitation:** v123 notice events will continue to queue in the private database without delivery. These are NOT claimed as delivered, and must not be silently treated as successful. Stripe native receipts and recovery emails are not equivalents for subscription cancellation/access-paused custom website notices. Review retention and future sender strategy separately; never replay old notifications without evaluating relevance and duplicate-send risk.
