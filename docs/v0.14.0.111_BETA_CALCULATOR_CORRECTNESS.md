## Four educational computation engines corrected

Existing `assets/js/advanced-tools.js` accepted the same risk budget for wrong-side stops and could return a share size that exceeded cash-equivalent account exposure. Blank number fields became zero because `Number('')===0`. Float ratios could silently use float greater than shares outstanding, and spread/execution tools accepted physically invalid quote and share inputs.

The reviewed calculations now live in `assets/js/advanced-tool-math-v014111.js` as pure stateless math, separate from the presentation code, and are loaded before the existing beta workbench script. The math unit test covers 21 invalid boundary scenarios plus valid formulas.

**Risk & Position Sizing:** Explicit LONG or SHORT, stop on loss side, positive inputs, nonnegative spread/slippage, target on prospective profit side, positive account and risk budget, per-share risk including planned friction, whole shares limited by both risk budget and 1× cash-equivalent exposure. Short borrow, fees, margin, real stop fills and gaps are NOT modeled.

**Float/Supply:** Reject positive float exceeding outstanding shares, negative volume, zero/negative quote, or invalid inputs; illustrate turnover, float percent, market capitalization and estimated float value with sourced-input disclaimer.

**Liquidity/Spread:** Reject crossed/inverted or negative quote, invalid/fractional share count and nonpositive stop distance; compute observed midpoint spread percentage, one-spread friction and ratio to stop-risk. No order-book depth, venue or latency modeling.

**Execution Cost:** Reject negative/blank decision/fill, zero/fractional shares, invalid direction and incomplete/crossed quotes. Compare observed input prices by LONG/SHORT sign. This is **not an order simulator**, proof of a fill, or real transactional fee calculation.

All results use DOM-safe text nodes rather than interpolating member fields into trusted HTML. Existing response workspace and historical market claims remain separately gated.

## Safe operational telemetry

`functions/_lib/tool-telemetry-v014111.js` reports only `beta_access`, `beta_workspace` and reserved `beta_calculation` categories and a strictly allowlisted reason code. Neither market symbols, prompts, Member IDs, session tokens, passwords, financial data, IP addresses, full requests, saved work nor private account identifiers are logged. It cannot replace a production log pipeline, alerting and incident triage.
