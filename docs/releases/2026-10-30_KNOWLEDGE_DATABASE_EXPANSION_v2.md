## Original teaching contract
Each new article supplies a plain-language definition, relevance, four reproducible method steps, original fictional numerical or decision case, risk limitation, original independent question, worked Talia comparison, unrestricted concept explanations, an original research-provenance disclosure, a public source primer and related existing routes. The user must write at least 25 characters to unlock answer comparison; free explanatory help is always available. No learner text is saved, graded or sold.

**Source-derived educational framing** includes market patterns, relative-volume evaluation, watchlist organization and trade-plan risk themes from the supplied research. **Independently verifiable background facts** include SEC order types and EDGAR disclosure categories. No causal or predictive claim is inferred from historical selected runners.

## ECharts numerical workshop
The first-party eight-bar OHLCV classroom dataset begins with one revealed completed bar; learners may reveal one additional bar or reset. An accessible current-only table displays closes, volume and approximate cumulative VWAP calculated from typical prices. This bar-typical-price approximation is not the same as trade-by-trade VWAP. No live data, identity, payment API, member record, historical provider or external analytics action is invoked. If ECharts is unavailable, the table and calculation remain usable.
