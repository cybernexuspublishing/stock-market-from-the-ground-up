## Acceptance states and rules
`research → draft → started → implemented → beta_candidate → beta_testing → retail_review → retail_accepted`. The Owner may record early progress and next steps. **Beta and retail acceptance require external, traceable gate evidence.** Any open item remains visible in Admin; a fully accepted item moves to the read-only accepted archive without erasing audit history.

Minimum common gates: source rights/review, GitHub main CI exact commit, deployed matching Cloudflare SHA, Supabase auth/RLS and migrations, real authenticated browser acceptance, protected entitlement/exclusion, Owner sign-off. Commercial products additionally require verified catalog parity/Cloudflare switches, Stripe TEST and appropriate LIVE-mode evidence, expiration/renewal/refunds, webhook signature/idempotency, charge/grant correspondence, and confirmed fulfillment. For Free/noncommercial items Stripe payment gates may be formally recorded as independently verified not applicable; never falsely mark as passed.

No paid LIVE transaction ≥$0.01 or ordinary member Lumen debit solely to manufacture evidence without specific Owner approval. A separate LIVE $0.00 diagnostic cannot prove paid checkout lifecycle. A live Stripe account, active Stripe Price, or a Cloudflare enabled variable is NOT proof of completed acceptance.
