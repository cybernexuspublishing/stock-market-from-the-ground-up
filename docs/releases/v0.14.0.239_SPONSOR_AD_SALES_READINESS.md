# v0.14.0.239 — Sponsor & Advertising Sales Readiness / Acceptance Ledger

**Project:** The Stock Market: From the Ground Up
**Release branch:** `main`
**Disposition:** QUOTE INTAKE OPEN; ALL PAID AD PLACEMENTS AND STRIPE CHECKOUT CLOSED.

This is a manual-quote, invoice-based business advertising sales platform. No unapproved sponsorship rate was invented, no Stripe Price was fabricated, and no real ad delivery was turned on.

## Implemented and deployed

1. Existing Supabase models retained: `advertisers`, `ad_campaigns`, `ad_creatives`, `ad_placements`, `ad_events`, `plan_ad_policies`. Existing records preserved.
2. Eleven configured placement codes identified. Five conventional content placements are present: Home, Store, Market Commons, Free Dashboard, Sample Labs. Six Sign In/Create Account side/bottom placements exist but are not eligible under the current signed-out plan's allowed-slot policy; they must be reviewed separately before sale.
3. Three public catalog packages: Site Sponsorship, Section Sponsorship, Educational Resource Sponsorship. Price is **NULL**, basis `quote`; no fabricated contract terms, amounts, inventory guarantees or performance figures.
4. Stripe products created and independently checked in both environments (6 total). **All 6 inactive**, `default_price=null`, metadata `launch_state=quote_only`, `checkout_enabled=false`. Mapped to package records.
5. Public `advertise.html` quote form backed by `/api/ad-sales`, with Cloudflare Turnstile, honeypot, server-side validation and a private inquiry queue. Contact data accessible only via server Owner authorization. Email fallback remains available.
6. Private `admin-advertising.html` Sponsor & Advertising Center linked from Admin. Supports list/review of inquiries, advertiser registration, price decisions, campaign drafts, written-copy creative drafts, mandatory financial-promotion and HTTPS destination review, Owner campaign decisions, and Stripe LIVE invoice verification of pre-existing paid invoices. Operations have an audit trail.
7. Owner in-app notification generated for incoming inquiries; no paid mail delivery required, and no contact PII included in notification content.
8. Server-side transaction guard blocks activating any scheduled/active campaign unless **Owner approved**, **independently verified LIVE payment evidence recorded**, flight dates valid, explicit payment/delivery switches enabled. Creative approval alone never enables placement.
9. Context-only creative endpoint `/api/ad-slot`, with server-verifiable member-tier and Lifetime/Owner ad-free checks (both subscription records and plan-grant records), approved placement/path whitelist, HTTPS-only destinations, required disclosure text, campaign cap counts, session/24-hour frequency checks, and nonbillable privacy-minimized impression/click samples.
10. Actual sponsor slots are placed outside educational decision content on Home, Store, Market Commons, Sample Labs, and Free Dashboard. No third-party scripts or pixels are permitted in the creative-renderer data path.
11. Public Advertise page corrected to identify Full Monthly/Annual as **limited sponsor** membership tiers; Lifetime and Owner remain ad-free, in agreement with current database access policies.
12. Campaign refund/dispute revocation SQL is installed. A later integration must connect verified webhook and/or Owner verification to this RPC before publishing customer-paid campaigns.

### Inviolable safety requirements

- Ads never enter Tutor answers, buy/wait/exit prompts, lab feedback or scoring, assessments, order entry, P&L, risk warnings or first-party rankings.
- Ad content must always be clearly identified as `Advertisement` or `Sponsored`; no ad targeting based on individualized financial decisions or protected personal data.
- Owner, Lifetime, and paid-entitlement exclusions must be enforced server-side and never inferred from client-supplied plan names.
- Draft, unpaid, rejected, unapproved, refunded, disputed, or expired campaigns must not display.
- No financial claim of guaranteed returns or paid influence over independent educational recommendations.
- No enabled LIVE ad billing or public campaign delivery without separate authorization.
