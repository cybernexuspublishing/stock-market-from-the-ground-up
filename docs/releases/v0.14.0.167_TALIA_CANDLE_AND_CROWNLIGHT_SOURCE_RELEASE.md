## Controlling decisions preserved

- Nine approved Learning Journey stages remain Start, Learn, Experiment, Predict, Practice, Assess, Review, Transfer and Results. READ/OBSERVE/DECIDE from an alternate illustrative lesson plan are educational activities, **not** silent changes to the controlled progression.
- Use the same shared Lab Engine across all 419 Lab identifiers and the 30-day Boot Camp; no 31st official competency.
- Existing official assessment keys, future price bars, paid-credit ledgers, real grants and entitlements remain server-owned.
- In the simulation, bullish body = Close − Open; bearish body = Open − Close. General size = absolute difference; upper wick = High − max(Open,Close), lower wick = min(Open,Close) − Low. Body/range 0/0 is undefined, whereas zero body divided by nonzero range is 0%.
- Completed OHLC cannot establish the order of High and Low. In particular, **never** say the high was reached before the low without the actual intrabar record.
