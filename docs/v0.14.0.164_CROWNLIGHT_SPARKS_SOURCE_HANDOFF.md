## Member experience

- Two-column illustrated chart and Talia on sufficiently wide desktop screens; stacked on smaller screens, including an embedded Lab from the 419-Lab Registry.
- Learning support options appear below both columns; the four free lesson-explanation buttons remain inside the visual and narrate through Talia.
- Source examples say **illustrative** or **simulated**, not **fictional**; simulated values are not presented as historical, licensed, or verified market observations.
- **Crownlight Sparks** is the consumer name. Existing `groundup_question_pass_*` database/API names remain stable for technical compatibility.
- One Spark allows an **optional** Reveal or Skip on one supported practice question after explicit confirmation. Free teaching, corrections, and retries never require or debit a Spark.
- Revealed or skipped answers count as **Study Only**, not independently correct assessment.

## Owner Account Management

The existing Account Management editor displays the Crownlight Sparks summary, an adjustment history, and **Add / Remove / Set Available Total** operations alongside source-aware membership, purchases, books, and tools. Each action requires a 10–500-character audit reason and Owner authority; staff, test, showcase, and own-account adjustments are denied by the Cloudflare endpoint. A successful change creates an append-only adjustment event and a central `admin_audit_log` record.

The server uses a per-member advisory transaction lock shared with practice spends; adjustment request IDs and per-question spend IDs support idempotency. Purchase provenance is not overwritten; Owner grants use a separate `manual` source. Included Sparks expire 60 days after their issuing billing-period end. Purchased and Owner-adjusted Sparks do not expire under this design.

## Commercial issuance still blocked

Neither uploaded prototype demo counts nor browser buttons may create real purchased or included balances. The production Stripe purchase-product mapping, approved included-allocation counts for each plan, paid purchase bundle pricing, invoice success/refund/cancellation event fulfillment, ownership of historic orders, and seller terms remain **unresolved**. Until those rules are approved and wired to verified issuer events, automated included credits, purchased-credit checkout, refunds and renewals are **not operational**.

The Owner may review/correct balances through the protected Admin action only after the migrations are installed and the Owner workflow is acceptance-tested. Do not advertise a working payment-based Sparks economy or declare paid launch accepted until issuer events and refunds are operational and verified.
