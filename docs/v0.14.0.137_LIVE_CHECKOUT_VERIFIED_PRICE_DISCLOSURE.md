## Defect found

The prior customer `checkout.html` Stripe Managed flow enabled **Continue to secure Stripe checkout** when the public readiness endpoint returned only `liveCheckoutAvailable:true`, but left the checkout page's order **amount empty**. A buyer could be redirected to the payment provider without first seeing the verified USD base price or the renewal period on the merchant's own review screen. The server's protected POST already rechecks the actual LIVE Stripe Price and database amount before creating a Session; the fix is to **also disclose those verified facts before consent**. This closes a UX, price-transparency and customer-consent gap; it does **not** claim tax or regulatory certification.

## Risks / engineering backlog

- A read-only readiness GET validates configured LIVE Stripe Price but **cannot prove Stripe account acceptance of `managed_payments[enabled]` during a real session POST**. This remains a hard acceptance boundary.
- The current checkout inserts a unique GU58 prepared intent for each submitted request; browser button disabling prevents ordinary double-clicks but does not provide atomic cross-device idempotency for simultaneous member sessions. A database-enforced per-member/SKU in-flight reservation plus safe session recovery would require a separate reviewed migration/contract and should be designed before high-volume release.
- Actual refunds, disputes and reconciliation recovery remain detect-only or provider-event-driven; do not grant/revoke from a read-only report.
- KDP books, signed physical books, future SKUs, learning curriculum, 419 labs and 30 competencies are not changed.
- The private billing reconciliation register currently has no paid recurring entries. A null ledger is **not** a green end-to-end payment integration status.
