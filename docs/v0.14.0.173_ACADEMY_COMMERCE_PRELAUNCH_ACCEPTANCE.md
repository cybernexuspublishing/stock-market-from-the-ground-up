## Activation must be an atomic verified release, not a naked button
1. Confirm course-specific source, lesson scripts, genuine historical rights where required, private answer keys, independent grading, four-dimensional competency evidence and accessibility acceptance.
2. Confirm product type and billing model (course purchase vs membership inclusion; no double-charging), final price and jurisdiction-specific tax classification. A generic site product must not silently grant all premium features.
3. Create appropriate Stripe Price for the exact environment and SKU, matching amount, currency and recurrence. Product + Price are separate objects; inactive Stripe catalog entries are non-sellable.
4. Install a server-owned immutable SKU -> Stripe environment-specific Price ID -> entitlement contract and enforce the *same* SKU validation at Checkout and signed webhook fulfillment. Never trust a client-submitted amount, status, entitlement or Stripe Price ID.
5. Confirm complete webhook handling: paid synchronous/asynchronous checkout, subscription renewal/failure/cancel, refunds, chargebacks, replay/idempotency, owner reconciliation, and revocation policy. No credit granted on redirect.
6. Require reviewed terms, privacy, refund/cancellation, educational disclosures and, for physical books, shipping.
7. Confirm legally applicable tax registration before turning on `automatic_tax`; divergent Stripe tax codes on recurring platform memberships require tax-specialist confirmation.
8. Run isolated test purchase, membership/credits/entitlement verification, multi-account isolation, missing-record/failure cases, accessibility and real browser acceptance.
9. Only then allow an authenticated Owner-controlled, audited switch with two-factor server readiness checks. The Owner has reserved LIVE payment activation. A mere disabled-to-enabled HTML control is insufficient.

## Founding 600 controls
- Founder subscriptions: Standard `$49.99 / 30 days`, Extended `$59.99 / 60 days`. They do NOT receive stacked percentage discounts on their own renewal rates.
- Preserved eligible founding discounts: 10% Standard, 20% Extended on other qualifying first-party purchases (subject to actual member eligibility and live safe coupon/checkout logic).
- Separately priced `$2,499` Lifetime purchase may qualify at `$2,249.10` and `$1,999.20` respectively; founder status and Lifetime entitlement are distinct.
- No automated double subscription charge after Lifetime upgrade. This requires explicit cancellation/renewal behavior validated before launch.
