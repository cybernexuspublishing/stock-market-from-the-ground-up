## Bar-by-bar verification contract

To label a displayed candle **Historical — verified**, the authorized historical-data registry and independent QA must record:

1. Canonical Lab ID; actual ticker and *security identity as of the historical date* (exchange, symbol-change and corporate-action chain). A later reused ticker is not sufficient.
2. Trading date, US/Eastern timezone, exact start/end instant and daylight-saving offset; session classification (premarket, regular, after-hours); requested base interval and precise bar start/end rules.
3. Feed/provider ID and scope: consolidated tape versus single-venue trades; eligible trade conditions, corrections/cancels, odd lots, coverage, adjustment policy and whether volume is traded shares for the same universe.
4. Independent source identifier and retrieval time; raw immutable sample checksum; verified Open, High, Low, Close, Volume for each visible bar, and confidence/exception notes. Check `low <= min(open,close) <= max(open,close) <= high` and `volume >= 0`.
5. Visible chart's VWAP/EMA indicators computed from documented eligible observations. A bar-close × volume approximation must not be advertised as tick-accurate market VWAP. Specify initialization and session boundaries.
6. Redistribution/embedding rights, mandatory acknowledgments, licenses and retention restrictions; avoid publishing authenticated exports just because a member can view them privately.
7. No-hindsight authorization: at pause T, release only bars ending at/before T; lock prediction/decision BEFORE revealing the next bar; keep answer keys and future market bars on the server.

Status transitions must be explicit: `UNVERIFIED` → `SOURCE_REVIEWED` → `BAR_VALUES_MATCHED` → `RIGHTS_APPROVED` → `HISTORICAL_VERIFIED`. Do not automatically promote a case based on a matching date, article screenshot or approximate dollar amount. The status belongs to each source/timeframe/bar set, not just the Lab title.

Use learner-facing language:
- Before verification: **“Practice chart — simulated prices. The historical Lab details are separate.”**
- After source verification and rights review: **“Historical prices — [ticker], [date], [HH:MM ET], [timeframe], source: [provider/feed].”**
- For a genuine historical pause with only an illustrative *calculation* layered over the chart: **“Historical chart; the worked arithmetic uses a separate teaching example.”**

Avoid the word “fictional” in member-facing captions but DO NOT remove the simulated/historical distinction.

## Low-cost source candidate and licensing boundary

- IEX Exchange HIST offers exchange-only historical feeds on T+1; its terms explicitly say that data does **not** include trading activity on other markets. Attribution and rights review are required when redistributing.
  - https://www.iex.io/products/market-data-connectivity
  - https://www.iex.io/legal/hist-data-terms
- NYSE Historical TAQ includes trade and quote products that can support granular reconstructions but requires the appropriate access and permitted use.
  - https://www.nyse.com/market-data/historical/
  - https://www.nyse.com/data-products/catalog/daily-taq/
- Do not treat IEX-only candle/volume as the full U.S. tape. Do not treat an unverified free third-party aggregation as automatically authorized for paid-member redistribution.
