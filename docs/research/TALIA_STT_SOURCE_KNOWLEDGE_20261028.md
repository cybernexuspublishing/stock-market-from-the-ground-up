## Source-constrained original material
`data/tutor-research-guides-v1.json` contains 32 original rewritten educational explainers, each with concept, decision procedure, limitations, invalidation conditions, common error, self-check, explicit archive provenance, and approved official educational reference URL where available. These are NOT source transcripts, trade recommendations, historical price replays, or claims of statistically validated performance.

`functions/_lib/original-competency-tutor-v014100.js` loads this optional static public source via the existing ASSETS route and adds research guides to the deterministic retrieval ranking, while retaining the canonical 30 official competency guides and existing single supplemental replay guide unchanged. An unavailable or malformed optional research asset falls back to the existing 30 guides.

`functions/api/market-tutor-v01492.js` identifies a research-informed topic as ungraded and preserves source metadata; authenticated Tutor entitlement, consent/quota and no-private-answers rules remain unchanged. No extra AI provider or paid inference is activated.

## Validation boundary
`tests/test_tutor_stt_research_guides_v1.mjs` checks 32 unique original topics, their fields and provenance, trusted external domains, focused retrieval across 22 subjects, 30-skill invariance, and graceful optional-catalog failure. It is wired into the existing Market Tutor Trust workflow.
