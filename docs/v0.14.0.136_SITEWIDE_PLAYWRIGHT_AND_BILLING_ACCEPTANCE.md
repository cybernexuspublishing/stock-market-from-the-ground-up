## Operational / change-control boundaries

This version is **QA infrastructure and release evidence**, not an activation or a new Stripe feature. In particular:

1. Keep current `ground-up-billing-scheduler`, daily `0 12 * * *` Cron, stable `/api/internal-billing-reconcile`, billing secret and redirect safety untouched.
2. Keep the eight approved LIVE Stripe Product/Price mappings and all existing customer entitlements, Stripe event ledgers, book/KDP content and pricing untouched.
3. Do **not** create a paid TinyFish automation run, a new Browser Harness instance, a new database, a new Stripe Price or a test charge.
4. Do **not** infer production readiness from `liveCheckoutAvailable:true`: it is the **noncharging GET** for a product, not proof of real `cs_live` checkout POST, signed `invoice.paid`, authenticated 200 webhook, correct entitlement and customer interface.
5. Future products are not automatically included by the eight-SKU GU58 mapping: product registration expansion requires separately reviewed schema/contract evolution, not bypassing the exact-eight guard.
