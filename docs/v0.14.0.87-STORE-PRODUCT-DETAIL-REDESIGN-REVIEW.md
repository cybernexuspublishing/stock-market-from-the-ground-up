**Purpose:** Fix the two specific Owner-reported public customer experience defects in the Store: the Historical Replay Monthly card lacks a *visible, near-price* details link, and every product page uses a split intro/price hero and disconnected equal-column cards that obscure product subject matter and create large blank regions.

## Visual and information architecture decision
Every membership, book and individually priced tool retains its own unique established URL and the single shared safe renderer. The redesigned page has:
1. A clear full-width product identity and subject-area summary at the top, with a large cover next to the title for books. The cover is no longer duplicated in the lower About section.
2. One primary reading column: detailed product description and verified curriculum subjects/outcomes, included features, excluded benefits, scope and billing/edition/access details. Each section flows immediately into the next without same-height marketing columns.
3. A secondary, narrower pricing-and-availability sidebar. On desktop it is sticky under the existing navigation; on narrow screens it follows the product's description and exclusions. The availability text never claims a live sale channel exists unless the canonical existing catalog supplies an eligible URL or checkout flag.
4. A wider responsive page container, comfortable reading length and sensible typography/line height, including browser text zoom and narrow viewports.

The primary subject is **what the customer receives and learns**, not price-first empty boxes. Changing the presentation must not change any customer entitlement or fulfillment behavior.

## Owner-specific correction
The `ONDEMAND_MONTHLY` 19.99/month card in `store.html#memberships` now displays **“What’s included, excluded & taught →”** immediately after its displayed database price and before the marketing description. It links to the **existing** `store-membership-ondemand-monthly.html`, whether or not enrollment is enabled. All other memberships get the same discoverable near-price link. The button at card bottom is retained; it no longer relies on flex margin-top auto to become visible.

The separate **coming-soon** Historical Replay training *preview* remains separate from the active ONDEMAND_MONTHLY paid product and never implies that the pending training-only program can be purchased.
