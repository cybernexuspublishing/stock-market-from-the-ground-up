## Priority definitions

P0: exploitability or correctness affecting access, payment, private data, or reliable educational outcomes; blocks activation or production acceptance. P1: major feature/operational deficiency with a safe gated fallback. P2: workflow, reliability, accessibility, and maintainability defects that should be remediated before public scale. Severity does not imply that static code changes have been verified on a live database.

## Legal / trading accuracy boundaries

Education, simulated execution, journal summaries and forum discussions are not trade recommendations or promises of profit. Brokerage fills and tape cannot be accurately reconstructed from OHLCV candles; hypothetical slippage, spreads and volume need explicit labeling and bounds. Do not reproduce KDP-exclusive enrolled eBook text on the companion site. Market data must be used within provider license and retention/redistribution terms. The compliance observations above are issues requiring appropriate licensed professionals where applicable, not legal advice or a legal opinion.
