Purchased-book account verification

Verify your book purchase

Free Membership is preserved for eligible verified book purchasers, although it is not offered as a general public membership selection. Verification also lets our staff activate the specific eligible book you purchased in your account.

Where to send your receipt

Send a copy of your purchase receipt to stockmarketfromthegroundup@gmail.com.

  1. Register or sign in. Create your website account using the same email address that will send the receipt, and confirm your registration email. Create an account.
  2. Send the email from your registered address. In the subject line use Book Purchase Verification. Messages from a different sender address cannot be approved until the registered identity has been verified.
  3. Attach the receipt. Include the purchased book title and edition, merchant or retailer, purchase date, and order/receipt reference. The image or PDF should establish the purchase without exposing unnecessary personal information.
  4. Allow three calendar days. Once we receive a complete, valid purchase receipt from your registered email, staff review eligibility and activate the verified book in your account within three calendar days. If verification is incomplete, you may be contacted for clarification.

Security and eligibility

Never send passwords, verification codes, full payment-card numbers or bank details. You may redact unrelated payment information, provided the proof still shows the eligible book, retailer, date and order reference. The receipt is reviewed; it does not instantly activate book rights or a paid Founding subscription.

The existing book-possession challenge and legacy Book Key redemption process are separate from receipt verification. We will not ask you to enter a retailer password on this website.

Free access does not include Founders Commons, Crownlight Sparks or paid Founding benefits unless you separately hold a qualifying membership.
