Purchased-book account verification

Verify your book purchase

Free Membership remains available for eligible verified book purchasers even though it is not a general membership selection. Submit a ticket in your account with the required purchase receipt attached.

Submit your receipt to Admin

  1. Register or sign in. Create your account and confirm your email. Create an account.
  2. Open Support. Go to the Dashboard Support section and choose Book Purchase Verification.
  3. Attach the receipt. Select the eligible book, enter the retailer and order reference if available, and upload a clear PDF, JPG or PNG purchase receipt (maximum 6 MB).
  4. Track the ticket. Admin reviews valid evidence within three calendar days. Requests for more information, approval and activation decisions appear in the same private Support conversation.

Open Book Verification Ticket

Private receipt handling

The receipt is stored privately and available only to authorized Admin reviewers. Redact unrelated card or banking information; never attach account passwords or verification codes. A receipt does not automatically grant access and does not prove ownership of a downloadable third-party ebook.

Approved claims grant eligible Free Membership and the verified book's companion learning access. They do not include paid Founding privileges, Crownlight Sparks or Founders Commons.

The legacy Book Key and book-possession challenge remain separate. Sending the receipt by email is not required.
