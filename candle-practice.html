First, learn what a candle records

An open is the first recorded price of an interval; a close is the last. The high and low mark extremes reached during that interval. The body runs from the open to close, while the wicks extend to the high and low when those lie beyond the body. A candle alone does not establish which extreme came first, what caused it, or how a future order would execute.
