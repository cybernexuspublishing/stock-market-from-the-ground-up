OWNER, ADMIN and other staff roles, plus the known James_Test identity, are excluded from acquisition counts. Unclassified profiles are not silently treated as new customers. If provider pagination fails, the report fails closed rather than showing incomplete totals.

Visitor acquisition (separate data source)

Consented public sessions, page views and navigation interest come from Google Analytics 4; Google search impressions and clicks come from Search Console. Neither provider's data can be joined to private Supabase member identities. This report never substitutes account registrations for visitors or constructs an unverified visitor-to-member conversion percentage.

For recent visitor counts and Google discovery, use the existing SMFGU Search & GA4 Watch and connected analytics reports. Reporting delay, consent choice and the new site's limited history can all affect coverage.
