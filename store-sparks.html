How Lumens work

Lumens can be used for eligible optional learning benefits and temporary ad-free access. Eligible ungraded practice reveals and skips use the current published Lumen price, displayed before confirmation. Standard explanations, guided examples and ordinary practice retries remain included. Reveals and skips do not award mastery.

Crownlight Lumens never expire. Temporary benefits end at their disclosed expiration time. A signed-in member account is required to redeem them. Other marketplace categories become redeemable only after prices, customer access, fulfillment, and server authorization are actually released.

Lumens cannot be exchanged for cash or trading funds. They are educational rewards, not cryptocurrency or financial instruments.
