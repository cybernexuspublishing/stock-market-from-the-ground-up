How to use your Sparks

Sparks can be used for eligible optional learning benefits and temporary ad-free access. Eligible ungraded practice reveals and skips use the current published Sparks price, displayed before confirmation. Standard explanations, guided examples and ordinary practice retries remain included. Reveals and skips do not award mastery.

Crownlight Sparks never expire. Temporary benefits end at the displayed expiration time. A member account is required. Other marketplace categories will become redeemable only as their server-authorized features are released.

Sparks cannot be exchanged for cash or trading funds. They are learning-platform credits, not cryptocurrency or financial instruments.
