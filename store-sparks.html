How to use your Sparks

One Spark may be spent to reveal or skip one eligible ungraded Practice question, after your confirmation. Free tutoring, corrections, examples, and retry opportunities are always available. A skip or revealed answer never counts toward assessed mastery.

Purchased Sparks do not expire under the approved policy. Included Sparks have separate expiry rules, which your wallet shows. You must have a member account to use Sparks.

Sparks cannot be exchanged for cash or trading funds. They are learning-platform credits, not cryptocurrency or financial instruments.
